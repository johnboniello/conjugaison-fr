## Full description

I built Conjugaison FR as a companion to French Dictée Practice, this time focused on verb conjugation rather than spelling.

The app covers seven tenses, introduced in three levels. Level one covers présent, imparfait, and futur simple. Level two adds passé composé. Level three adds conditionnel présent and subjonctif présent. Impératif is included as well.

Three practice modes are included. Conjugue gives the verb, tense, and person, and asks for the correct form to be typed in. En contexte fills a blank in a sentence, where a time cue in the sentence signals which tense to use. Choix multiple presents the correct form among a set of choices.

Verbs to practice can be chosen directly, so a session can focus on a specific list rather than a random mix.

The app runs entirely on the device. There is no internet access required, no camera use, no accounts, no ads, and no tracking of any kind. Everything stays on your device.

I built this as a straightforward tool for real practice, without any of the extra layers that usually come with an app. I hope it is useful.
