# Conjugaison FR — French verb conjugation practice

Companion to [Dictée FR](https://github.com/johnboniello/french-dictee). Same
feel: chime + "Bravo !" on a correct answer, a soft tone + "Essaie encore" on a
wrong one; "avec aide" scoring when a hint is used.

## Levels

Pick a level on the home screen — it sets which tenses appear:

| Level | Tenses |
|---|---|
| 1 | présent · imparfait · futur simple |
| 2 | + passé composé |
| 3 | + conditionnel présent · subjonctif présent |
| 4 | + impératif |

## Modes

1. **Conjugue** — the verb, tense and person are given; type the form
   (`manger · imparfait · nous` → `mangions`). Wrong answers don't reveal the
   spelling; a 👁 Réponse button appears after 3 tries. 💡 Indice reveals letters.
2. **En contexte** — a sentence with a blank and a time cue that signals the
   tense; the infinitive is shown after the blank. Type the conjugated form.
3. **Choix multiple** — the verb, tense and person are given; pick the correct
   form from four. The wrong options are other real conjugations of the same
   verb (wrong person or wrong tense).

**Choisir les verbes** lets you pick which of the ~155 verbs are in rotation.

## Data

`data/verbs.json` — conjugations for ~155 common verbs, generated by
`data/gen.py` from Verbiste (GPL v2+). See `data/NOTICE.md`.
