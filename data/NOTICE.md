# Conjugation data

`verbs.json` is generated by `gen.py` from **Verbiste**:

- `verbs-fr.xml`, `conjugations-fr.xml` — © 2003–2016 Pierre Sarrazin,
  distributed under the GNU General Public License v2 or later.
  <http://sarrazip.com/dev/verbiste.html>
- English glosses added by Brett Tolbert (verbecc project).

`gen.py` applies the Verbiste templates to a curated list of ~155 common verbs
and writes the resulting forms (présent, imparfait, futur simple, conditionnel
présent, subjonctif présent, impératif, passé composé) as JSON.

To regenerate: `python3 gen.py` (needs `verbs-fr.xml` and `conjugations-fr.xml`
in the same folder).
